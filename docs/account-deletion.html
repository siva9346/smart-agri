Account & Data Deletion

Naveena Uzhavan (Smart Agri) · Last updated: 22 July 2026

You can request deletion of your Naveena Uzhavan account and all associated personal data at any time, directly from the app.

How to Request Deletion

  1. In the app (recommended): Open Naveena Uzhavan, go to Profile → Delete Account, read the warning, type DELETE to confirm, and submit. You will be signed out immediately and your account will be scheduled for deletion.
  2. By email: If you can no longer access the app, email sivavelmurugan8@gmail.com from your registered contact details with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered phone number, so we can locate your account and process the request on your behalf.
Retention period: 30 days. Once a deletion request is submitted, your account is deactivated immediately. Your data is then permanently and irreversibly deleted after a 30-day retention period. This window exists to protect against accidental or unauthorized deletion requests — contact us during this time if you'd like to cancel the request and keep your account.

What Gets Deleted

After the 30-day retention period, the following is permanently deleted:

  • Your name, phone number, email, and login credentials
  • Your land records
  • Your crop cycles and cultivation history
  • Your daily activity records, expense entries, and growth updates
  • Photos you uploaded
  • Your expert advisory requests and support enquiries

What May Be Retained

Your order/purchase history (items, amounts, and dates) is retained in a de-identified form for accounting and legal record-keeping — your identifying details (customer reference, delivery address, notes) are stripped and replaced with a non-identifying placeholder so it can no longer be linked back to you. No other personal data is retained beyond the 30-day window.

Questions, or want to cancel a pending deletion request? Contact sivavelmurugan8@gmail.com.
